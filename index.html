Why now

Nearshoring is opening plants faster than know-how can follow

Regulated manufacturers face two costly problems at once — and both worsen as production moves to Mexico and the Americas.

Know-how walks out the door

Process knowledge lives in a few expert operators. With 30–50% annual turnover, plants retrain constantly and lose expertise every time someone leaves.

Compliance is manual and relentless

Proving GMP / FDA / COFEPRIS compliance eats enormous effort — paper SOPs, clipboard audits, and evidence assembled by hand before every inspection.

The cameras are already there

Plants already run IP cameras on the floor. Computer vision plus LLMs finally make it possible to capture, guide and document work directly — no new hardware fleet.

The platform

Three modules. One intelligence layer for the line.

Everything runs on-premise on edge servers. Only metadata reaches the cloud — the video stays inside the plant, essential for IP-sensitive, regulated factories.

MODULE 01

KnowBase

● Live in pilots

Records expert operators on video and automatically generates structured SOPs and training material — steps, times, acceptance criteria. Onboards new operators in days, not months.

How it works

We close the whole loop

Competitors analyze work. IQ Edge AI captures the expertise, writes the SOP, validates against it live, and produces the audit trail — turning each plant's know-how into a structured, machine-readable asset.

Compliance evidence is produced as a byproduct of operating — not scrambled before an audit. Over time, the SOP + expert-video library becomes the structured knowledge base a plant can later use to deploy robots without starting from zero.
